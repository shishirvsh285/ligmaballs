Our Story

We Don't Just
Take Photos —
We Preserve Souls

Founded in 2012, Lumière Studio was born from a simple belief: that every genuine emotion deserves to be immortalised. We blend photojournalistic instinct with fine-art sensibility to deliver images that don't just document — they breathe.

Our team of four seasoned photographers has shot everything from intimate garden weddings to 3,000-person corporate galas, always with the same obsessive attention to light and story.
